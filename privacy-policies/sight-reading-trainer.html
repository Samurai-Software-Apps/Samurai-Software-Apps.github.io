Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sight Reading Trainer ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Sight Reading Trainer does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, or email address.

The app stores your practice settings and progress locally on your device. The developer does not have access to this information.

Depending on how you use the app, third-party services may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

Advertising

The free version of the app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy to provide and improve advertising services.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

The app may offer optional premium features through Google Play Billing. Payments are securely processed by Google. The developer does not receive or store your payment card information.

Analytics and Diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostic information may be collected to improve the app's performance, reliability, and stability.

How Information Is Used

  • Provide the app's educational features.
  • Save your practice preferences locally.
  • Improve app performance and stability.
  • Fix bugs and technical issues.
  • Display optional advertisements.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by trusted third-party providers such as Google Play and Google AdMob solely to provide their respective services.

Children's Privacy

Sight Reading Trainer is suitable for musicians of all ages. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
